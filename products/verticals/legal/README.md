# Barya, Byamugisha & Co. Advocates — Internal Legal Practice Platform

A1OS vertical product for the firm's governed internal practice operations.

Customer-facing name: **Barya, Byamugisha & Co. Advocates — Internal Legal Practice Platform**

Internal factory identifier: `legal-os`

This vertical covers matter management, clients, diary/deadlines, documents, billing, research and audit, with matter-level confidentiality enforced by the platform architecture.

The current frontend is the production UI foundation. Supabase schema/auth/RLS and external integrations are part of the connected production build.
