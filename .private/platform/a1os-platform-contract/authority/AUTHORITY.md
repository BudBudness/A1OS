# Authority Model

## Owner

Full platform visibility and authority.

## Organization

Controls its own configured resources and capabilities within platform policy.

## Identity

Human, service, device or API identity used to access a resource.

## Policy

Determines what an identity or workflow may perform.

## Workflow

Deterministic executable business/process logic.

## AI

Interpretation and recommendation layer only.

## Consequence classes

SAFE:
automatic execution permitted by policy.

REVIEW:
requires owner/authorized approval.

CRITICAL:
requires explicit authority and additional verification.

FORBIDDEN:
never executable through ordinary automation.
